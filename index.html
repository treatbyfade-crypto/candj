Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about C&J membership and earnings.

How do I start earning?
Register an account, activate your membership by paying N35,000, and wait for admin approval. Once approved, you earn N1,000 every 24 hours automatically.
How do withdrawals work?
Select a withdrawal tier, add your bank details, and submit a request. Admin reviews and pays out the net amount (65% of requested) after a 35% service fee.
What are referral points?
You earn 10 points for every friend who registers AND activates their membership using your code. 20 points are required to renew your membership for free.
Can I renew without paying?
Yes! If you have 20 referral points (from 2 active referrals), you can renew your membership for another 30 days without paying the N35,000 fee again.
Is my money safe?
Absolutely. All transactions are manually reviewed by our admin team. Your account data, balance, and earnings are securely stored and protected.

C&J LIMITED Support Center

Everything you need to know about using C&J LIMITED

Welcome To C&J LIMITED

C&J LIMITED is a digital platform that provides members with membership, automatic app earning, referral, wallet, community, and other digital platform features.

This Support Center provides basic information to help you understand and use the available C&J LIMITED features.

How To Create An Account

  • Open the C&J LIMITED homepage.
  • Select Register.
  • Enter your required registration information.
  • Create your account password.
  • Submit the registration form.

After successful registration, you can log in and access your C&J LIMITED account.

How To Login

  • Open the C&J LIMITED login page.
  • Enter your registered email address.
  • Enter your password.
  • Select Login.

After successful login, you will be taken to your account dashboard.

Dashboard

Your dashboard is the main area for managing and viewing your C&J LIMITED account.

  • View your available app balance.
  • View your current automatic earning.
  • View referral information.
  • Check membership status.
  • Access withdrawal features.
  • Access your profile and account settings.

Membership Activation

The current initial C&J LIMITED membership activation price is 35,000 for 30 days.
  • Open the Membership page.
  • Select Pay with Bank and transfer N35,000 to OPay account 6569097252, account name CLINTON AIRENOGBUAN.
  • Upload your payment receipt in the activation form and submit the request.
  • Alternatively, select ACTIVATE MEMBERSHIP FROM WALLET where the wallet activation option is available.
  • Wait for administrator verification and approval.

Membership does not become active simply because a payment receipt has been submitted. The request must be approved.

Automatic Earning

After membership has been successfully approved and activated, the account may begin receiving the current C&J automatic app earning.

The current earning rate is 1,000 per 24 hours.

The earning displayed inside the application is a virtual C&J app credit. The earning system does not require users to complete tasks, jobs, or work assignments.

C&J App Balance

The balance displayed inside C&J LIMITED represents virtual C&J app credits used within the platform.

It should not be interpreted as a bank deposit, guaranteed cash balance, investment, security, or other regulated financial product.

C&J LIMITED is not a bank or banking institution.

Referral Program

Every member may receive a unique referral link or referral code.

  • Share your referral link with others.
  • Registered referrals are users who join using your referral code.
  • Active referrals are registered users who activate membership.
  • Inactive referrals are registered users who have not activated membership.
  • Each active referral currently contributes 10 referral points.

Membership Renewal

The initial membership period is 30 days.

The current membership renewal requirement is 20 referral points.

Referral points are based on eligible active referrals according to the current C&J LIMITED membership rules.

Wallet System

Your C&J wallet allows you to view and use supported platform wallet features.

  • View available wallet balance.
  • Request eligible membership activation from your wallet.
  • View supported transaction information.
  • Access eligible withdrawal features.
  • Use supported wallet functions.

Games & Entertainment

Open the Games page from the navigation and choose a game card to start. Available games include Lucky Pick, Match Prediction, Try Your Luck, and Question & Answer.

These are free-play games for entertainment only. They do not use or award money or C&J points.

Please play responsibly. Game results are for entertainment only.

Deposits & Payments

Where a deposit or payment is required, use only the official payment information provided inside the C&J LIMITED application.

  • Enter the required amount.
  • Select the payment option.
  • Review the payment instructions.
  • Complete the payment.
  • Submit the required payment receipt.

A submitted payment remains pending until it is reviewed and approved by an administrator.

Withdrawals

  • Open the Withdraw page.
  • Make sure your eligible bank account information is saved.
  • Select an available withdrawal amount.
  • Enter your withdrawal PIN.
  • Review the withdrawal information.
  • Submit the withdrawal request.

Withdrawal requests are manually reviewed and processed according to the current C&J LIMITED withdrawal rules.

Bank Account

Eligible withdrawal requests require the correct bank account information.

  • Open Bank Account from the appropriate account section.
  • Enter your bank name.
  • Enter your account name.
  • Enter your account number.
  • Save your bank account information.

Always make sure your bank information is accurate before submitting a withdrawal request.

Wallet Transfers

Where wallet transfer functionality is available, members may transfer supported C&J app credits to another eligible C&J LIMITED member.

  • Open the Transfer feature.
  • Enter the recipient's required account information.
  • Enter the amount.
  • Review the transaction.
  • Confirm using the required security verification.

C&J Coin

C&J Coin is a virtual platform feature available within the C&J LIMITED application.

Users can view their stored wallet balance and manage available C&J Coin features according to the current platform rules.

C&J Coin values displayed inside the application are virtual app credits and should not be interpreted as guaranteed real-world financial assets.

C&J Gold

C&J Gold is another virtual platform feature available within C&J LIMITED.

Available purchase, holding, and buyback options are controlled by the current C&J LIMITED platform rules.

Community

The C&J LIMITED community allows members to interact and share content with other users.

  • Create posts.
  • Share approved content.
  • Like posts.
  • Comment and interact.
  • View member profiles.
  • Discover community content.

Profile

Your profile contains your personal account information and available profile details.

  • View your profile.
  • Manage available profile information.
  • Upload or update your profile picture where supported.
  • View relevant registered account details.

Notifications

Notifications may keep you informed about important account and platform activity.

  • Referral activity.
  • Wallet activity.
  • Withdrawal updates.
  • Membership updates.
  • Verification updates.
  • Important platform announcements.

Account Security

Protect your account by keeping your password, Transfer PIN, and other security information private.

  • Never share your password.
  • Never share your Transfer PIN.
  • Use only official C&J LIMITED support channels.
  • Contact support if you notice suspicious account activity.

Frozen or Disabled Account

C&J LIMITED may temporarily freeze or disable an account when necessary for security, verification, investigation, or policy enforcement.

While an account is frozen or disabled, certain features such as automatic earning, withdrawals, transfers, and other platform functions may be restricted.

Account restrictions do not automatically delete stored account balance, earnings history, referrals, transactions, profile information, or other account records.

If an account is restored, applicable platform functions may resume according to the current platform rules. No automatic earning catch-up is guaranteed for the period of restriction.

Account Verification

C&J LIMITED may require account or transaction verification before certain features or requests can be completed.

Verification helps protect accounts, confirm transactions, and reduce unauthorized or fraudulent activity.

Need Help?

If you experience problems with registration, login, membership, payments, wallet activity, bank account information, withdrawals, referrals, or account security, please contact the official C&J LIMITED Support team.

Use the official WhatsApp Admin or support channels provided inside the application.

Important Information

C&J LIMITED is a digital platform and is not a bank or banking institution.

Balances and earnings displayed within the application are virtual C&J app credits. Platform features, requirements, limits, and procedures may be updated from time to time.

Always follow the latest instructions displayed inside the C&J LIMITED application.

Contact C&J LIMITED

For account assistance, payment verification, membership, withdrawals, referrals, technical issues, or other platform-related questions, contact the official C&J LIMITED support team through the official support channels provided inside the application.
